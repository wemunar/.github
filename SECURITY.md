# Security policy

## Reporting a vulnerability

Please report a suspected vulnerability **privately**, by e-mail to `security@wemunar.com`. Do not open a public issue or pull request for it.

Include what you found, where, the steps to reproduce it and the impact you see. Do not include other people's personal data.

We send a first response within **5 business days** and tell you what we found and what happens next. We ask you to give us a reasonable time to fix the issue before you disclose it, and we will credit you if you want to be named.

## Scope and supported versions

Our products are pre-launch: only the latest `main` of each repository is supported. Reports about third-party services we use belong to those services.

## What we do not accept

Automated scanner output without a demonstrated impact, social engineering of staff, and denial-of-service testing against production.
